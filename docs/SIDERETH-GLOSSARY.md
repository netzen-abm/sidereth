# SIDERETH Canonical Glossary V1

## Purpose
A shared vocabulary for product, engineering, legal-domain modelling, UX and AI/agent work.

## Core terms

**Case** — The universal record representing a legal/regulatory matter across its lifecycle.

**Incident** — A time-bounded or event-driven official interaction or occurrence that may form part of a Case.

**Event** — An immutable, timestamped fact describing a material state change or recorded occurrence.

**Evidence** — Information preserved to support or verify a fact, event, claim or legal proposition. Original evidence is immutable.

**Document** — A structured or unstructured record received, generated or referenced by a Case or Incident.

**Legal Source** — An authoritative or otherwise explicitly classified source used to support a legal proposition or procedural statement.

**Provenance** — The traceable relationship between a proposition, artifact or decision and its originating source(s), transformation(s) and retrieval context.

**Jurisdiction** — The legally relevant geographic, subject-matter, personal, territorial or institutional scope governing a matter.

**Authority** — The institution, office or actor empowered to perform a defined function under an applicable legal framework.

**Procedure** — The sequence of legally or administratively relevant steps governing an action or matter.

**Deadline** — A time constraint derived from a verified rule, official document, procedural instruction or explicitly identified user requirement.

**Capability** — A reusable system function exposed through shared infrastructure and bounded by a capability contract.

**Adapter** — A surface-specific integration that invokes shared capabilities without reimplementing their legal/business semantics.

**Agent** — Software that can execute bounded multi-step workflows using approved tools under policy and authorization controls.

**Tool Gateway** — The enforcement boundary through which tool execution passes identity, policy, permission, data-minimisation and audit checks.

**High-impact action** — An action that can materially affect legal rights, obligations, proceedings, submissions, external communications or other consequential outcomes and therefore requires defined human approval.

**Human assistance** — Routing to an appropriately qualified human professional or legal-aid pathway when automation is insufficient or required by risk.

**Verified rule** — A legal proposition supported by an identified authoritative source with sufficient provenance and effective-date context.

**Inference** — A reasoned conclusion derived from facts and sources that is not itself directly stated by an authority.

**Uncertainty** — A condition in which the available sources, facts or interpretation are insufficient to establish a proposition confidently.

## Required distinctions
1. User facts are not legal authority.
2. An official procedure is not automatically the same thing as a statutory rule.
3. AI output is not a legal source.
4. A draft is not a submitted legal action.
5. An event is not the same thing as the current derived state.
6. A document analysis is not evidence unless its evidentiary status is explicitly established.
