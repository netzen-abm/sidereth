# The Purple Frog — Conservation Awareness Foundation

**Status:** FOUNDATIONAL PRODUCT PRINCIPLE
**Decision date:** 2026-09-06

## Purpose

The Purple Frog uses technology to build awareness around threatened life and support informed protection of threatened species.

The initial emblem is the **Gaur / Indian Bison (*Bos gaurus*)**, a **Vulnerable (VU)** species. The Gaur is an ambassador for the conservation mission; it is not presented as Critically Endangered.

## Mission statement

> **Awareness of a Vulnerable species. Protection of threatened species. Respect for every species.**

The mission includes awareness and protection efforts concerning:

- Vulnerable species
- Endangered species
- Critically Endangered species
- Other threatened species
- Threatened habitats and ecosystems

## Why begin with a Vulnerable species?

Conservation awareness should not begin only when a species reaches the most severe categories. A Vulnerable species is already facing an elevated risk of extinction in the wild. The Gaur therefore provides a scientifically accurate starting point for a broader public conversation about prevention, protection and responsible action.

The product should communicate a progression:

```text
Awareness
    ↓
Understanding
    ↓
Concern
    ↓
Responsible action
    ↓
Protection
    ↓
Recovery / long-term resilience
```

This is a product philosophy, not a claim that technology alone can produce conservation outcomes.

## Conservation-status integrity

Conservation categories are sourced facts, not branding adjectives. Every public status claim must preserve:

1. scientific name
2. common name
3. conservation category
4. assessment scope
5. assessment date/version where available
6. authoritative source
7. source-data licensing/usage requirements

The system must never infer that the Gaur's Vulnerable status applies to another species, region, population or assessment without evidence.

## Species-awareness resource model

The long-term shared infrastructure should model conservation information as structured resources:

```text
Species
  ├── Taxonomy
  ├── Common names
  ├── Conservation status
  ├── Assessment
  ├── Geographic range
  ├── Habitat
  ├── Threats
  ├── Population evidence
  ├── Protection measures
  ├── Conservation actions
  ├── Sources
  └── Media / usage rights
```

These resources should be reusable across web, mobile, messaging, educational and future product surfaces rather than being duplicated in individual interfaces.

## Public-awareness design principles

### 1. Accuracy before impact
Do not exaggerate conservation status to make a message more emotionally powerful.

### 2. Species dignity
Avoid reducing a species to a mascot. Present animals and ecosystems as living subjects with ecological significance.

### 3. Actionability
Where appropriate, awareness should connect to lawful, practical and evidence-based actions.

### 4. Local relevance
Species information should be contextualized by geography, habitat and applicable conservation framework.

### 5. Source visibility
Users should be able to distinguish verified source information from educational interpretation or product-generated content.

### 6. No false certainty
AI-generated conservation explanations must not silently upgrade, downgrade or invent conservation status.

### 7. Human responsibility
High-impact conservation decisions remain subject to qualified human judgment and applicable law.

**Common name:** Gaur / Indian Bison  
**Scientific name:** *Bos gaurus*  
**Global conservation status used by the product:** Vulnerable (VU)  
**Role in The Purple Frog:** Initial conservation ambassador  
**Meaning:** Awareness of a Vulnerable species and an entry point to broader protection of threatened species.

The product should not use the phrase “Critically Endangered Gaur.”

## Source policy

The product should prioritize authoritative conservation assessments and official government conservation sources. Wikipedia and educational/reference websites can assist discovery and public education, but should not be treated as the sole authority for dynamic conservation-status claims.

For the Gaur, the current foundation verification has been cross-checked against authoritative conservation/government material, including the IUCN Red List classification and Indian government conservation sources.

## Future capability

A future shared **Species Awareness Capability** may provide:

- sourced species profiles
- conservation-status timelines
- threat and habitat explanations
- regional awareness
- educational cards
- evidence-linked stories
- conservation-action guidance
- source/version tracking
- multilingual awareness content
- AI-assisted explanation with provenance and policy controls

This capability should remain independent of any single UI or AI provider and should be consumable by every approved product surface.
