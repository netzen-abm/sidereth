# SIDERETH

**Legal & Regulatory Infrastructure**

SIDERETH is a privacy-first, modular legal and regulatory workflow platform designed to help people and businesses navigate the rule of law.

## What it does

- Prepare applications and compliance workflows
- Check jurisdiction, authority, procedure and documentary readiness
- Protect and record lawful government interactions
- Preserve evidence and build verifiable timelines
- Understand notices, orders and decisions
- Track deadlines and required actions
- Draft responses for user review
- Escalate unresolved matters
- Route high-stakes matters to qualified human professionals

## Architecture principle
The platform is built around shared, domain-independent capabilities. Web, mobile and messaging surfaces are replaceable clients over the same contracts and engines.

## AI principle
AI is optional intelligence. It may retrieve, classify, summarize, organize, draft and recommend within policy boundaries. It does not become the source of law and does not autonomously perform high-impact legal actions.

## Privacy principle
Local-first by default. Sensitive case data should remain under user control. External processing is minimized, explicit, encrypted and auditable.

## Initial engineering milestone
Build the domain-independent Case and Incident Engine first. Then integrate Panchayat and Municipality domain adapters without duplicating shared infrastructure.
