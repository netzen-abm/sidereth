# The Purple Frog / SIDERETH — Commercial EULA Framework

**Status:** DRAFT / LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED  
**Decision date:** 2026-09-17

This document is a structural framework for a future commercial End User License Agreement. It is **not a final legal agreement** and must be reviewed and adapted by qualified counsel before commercial use.

## 1. Scope

The final EULA should apply only to software, services, distributions or extensions expressly identified as commercial/proprietary.

It must not purport to relicense SIDERETH components already distributed under Apache-2.0 or AGPL.

## 2. License grant

Define the limited, non-exclusive commercial right to access, install, execute and use the identified proprietary product/service within the purchased scope.

## 3. Ownership

State that proprietary software, commercial extensions, trademarks and other protected materials remain owned by their respective rights holders except for rights expressly granted.

## 4. Open-source components

The EULA must expressly identify that components distributed under Apache-2.0, AGPL or other third-party licenses remain governed by those licenses. Where terms conflict, the applicable open-source license controls the rights it grants.

## 5. User responsibilities

Address lawful use, credential protection, authorized users, security hygiene, device compatibility, prohibited misuse and compliance with applicable law.

## 6. Privacy and data

Reference the applicable privacy notice/data-processing terms and define controller/processor responsibilities where relevant. Do not use the EULA to silently expand data collection beyond the product's privacy architecture.

## 7. AI and agent terms

Where AI/agents are included, define:

- permitted uses;
- human-review requirements;
- prohibited high-impact autonomous use;
- provider/model limitations;
- data handling;
- output limitations;
- audit/logging terms where applicable.

## 8. Hardware/device integrations

Where hardware is supported, identify supported device classes and clarify that device permissions, operating-system behavior and manufacturer terms may impose additional conditions.

## 9. Support and service levels

Define support channels, service levels, maintenance windows, update policy and any paid support obligations.

## 10. Warranties and disclaimers

Address warranties permitted by applicable law and clearly state limitations without attempting to exclude rights that cannot legally be excluded.

## 11. Liability

Define appropriate limitation-of-liability language, exclusions and any jurisdiction-specific mandatory rights.

## 12. Termination

Define termination events, cure periods where applicable, effects of termination, credential/service suspension and treatment of customer data.

## 13. Governing law

The final agreement must specify governing law, dispute resolution and jurisdiction after legal review of the intended market and contracting entity.

## 14. Trademark

The Purple Frog name and combined Gaur + Purple Frog emblem require separate trademark/brand-use rules. Commercial users should not receive implied rights to modify, register, imitate or misuse the brand.

## 15. Required companion documents

A production commercial release should have, as applicable:

- final EULA;
- privacy notice;
- data-processing agreement;
- security terms;
- support/SLA;
- third-party/open-source notices;
- trademark/brand-use policy;
- acceptable-use policy;
- export/sanctions terms where required.
