## Conservation integrity

The Gaur / Indian Bison (*Bos gaurus*) is represented as **Vulnerable (VU)**. The brand mission extends to awareness and protection of threatened species, including Endangered and Critically Endangered species. The logo itself must not imply that Gaur is Critically Endangered.
